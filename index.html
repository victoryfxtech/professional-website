ABOUT ME

Focused on creating solutions, not just designs.

I'm Alex Morgan, a designer and developer based in San Francisco. I combine strategic thinking, visual design, and front-end development to build digital products that solve real problems and delight users.

With 6+ years of experience, I've worked with startups, established brands, and everything in between. I believe good design should make things easier, and every pixel should serve a purpose.

When I'm not designing or coding, you'll find me exploring new tools, reading about user behavior, or contributing to open-source projects.
